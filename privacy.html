Who's Stronger?

Privacy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Who's Stronger? is a free daily game. You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name or email.

What stays on your device

Your answers, scores, streak and settings (such as sound on/off) are saved in your browser's local storage, so you can come back tomorrow and keep your streak. This data never leaves your device unless noted below. Clearing your browser data removes it.

What is sent to our server

To show how other players voted and how your score compares, the game sends anonymous results:

  • which fighter you picked in each daily fight, and your guess in slider rounds;
  • your total score for the day;
  • a random ID created in your browser, so the same browser is counted once per fight.

This contains no name, email or account. We don't store your IP address with the votes. Results are only used as totals ("62% of players picked Goku").

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't track you across sites. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Cookies

The game doesn't set cookies. If we add ads later, this page will be updated first and you will be asked for consent where the law requires it.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of a normal web request.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Characters and pictures

Who's Stronger? is an unofficial fan project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any rights holder. All characters, names and images belong to their respective owners. If you own the rights to something shown here and want it removed, write to us and we will take it down.

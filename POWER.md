# Power ranking: all 100 fighters

The win chance in the game comes from this ranking (column `power` in `data/characters.csv`).
Rated version: anime = peak in anime/manga; Marvel, DC, The Boys, Invincible = film or series version when there is one.

How to read the gap: 35 points ≈ 60%, 70 ≈ 70%, 100 ≈ 76%, 150 ≈ 85%, 200 ≈ 91%.
"Wiki tier" is the highest tier on the VS Battles Wiki page of that version (often inflated by gear or one-off forms). Full report: `data/vsbw-report.csv`.

Hand-judged fights (data/matchups.csv) can beat raw power when weapons or abilities decide the fight; the build prints which ones do.

To change who beats whom: edit `power` (and `tier` if needed) in the table, then `node tools/build-data.js`.
